About Manpower Projects & Polymer

Manpower Projects & Polymer Pvt. Ltd. is a leading infrastructure company specializing in Bridge Rehabilitation, Structural Repairs, Expansion Joints, Waterproofing Systems, Steel Fabrication, Building Construction and Architectural Engineering.

With over 30 years of experience, the company has successfully completed prestigious projects for Delhi Metro, L&T, Nepal Government, UP Bridge Corporation, World Trade Centre Noida, Rajasthan Toll Plaza, Bihar Rajya Pul Nirman Nigam Ltd., and many other government and private organizations.

Our mission is to provide innovative engineering solutions that exceed client expectations while maintaining the highest standards of quality, reliability and safety.

Today, Manpower Projects & Polymer has earned a strong reputation for delivering durable, cost-effective and technically advanced infrastructure solutions across India and internationally.
